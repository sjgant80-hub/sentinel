What v2 hardened

Four flags a verify pass raised, each now sealed and measured, not asserted.

A bounded replay store

An unbounded set of every nonce ever seen is a memory-exhaustion DoS. The store is now a sliding window of the most recent 64 nonces. Under a flood of 400 unique packets it peaked at 64 — never past the cap — while 64 of 64 in-window replays were still caught. A packet older than the window is forgotten; that trade is what bounds the memory.

Gate and detector, coupled

The per-packet gate has no cumulative budget. A stream of 20 packets each within the per-packet cap of 3000 drew 59810 cumulatively — 20× the cap. Every packet passed the gate; the detector over the accepted window caught the drain. Gate and detector together, not either alone.

A harder held-out set

The clean train/test split gave the detector a fat margin. Re-measured on a near-boundary set — legit and hostile mixed right at the cut, a real distribution shift — the grown detector held up: 0 of 32 caught vs the baseline's 0, no legit stream flagged (the best any threshold on its weights reaches here is 20).

A contained honeypot

Every one of the 72 rerouted attacks got 0 budget and 0 capabilities, and 0 escalation pivots succeeded. A dead-end that is measured to be a dead-end.

The 6-byte packet

A command is six bytes — the Primorial-Fold packet. On the wire it travels signed: [sourceId:1][payload:6][Ed25519 signature:64] = 71 bytes.

[0] opcodewhat to do
[1] source · targettwo 4-bit node indices
[2] resourcesan 8-bit capability bitmask
[3–4] budgetuint16, little-endian
[5] κ-witnessthe primorial fold of bytes 0–4

Seven defences, one shape

verify before parse

The Ed25519 signature over sourceId+payload is checked before the six bytes are ever unpacked. A forged or tampered command is dropped before it reaches a DataView — an un-verified command never becomes a command.

budget ≤ the lattice

Each source is granted a capability: a maximum budget and an allowed set of resources. A command cannot spend budget it was never granted, or touch a resource outside its grant.

at κ

The sixth byte is the primorial fold of the first five (Thomas Frumkin's codec). A tampered payload no longer folds to its witness, so it reads off-κ without anyone needing to know the attack.

unseen

The signature is the packet's nonce; the same signed packet twice is a replay and is dropped.

the honeypot

A rejected command is dropped into a zero-budget phantom cell that touches no real state — an attacker who keeps probing only ever learns the shape of a cell that was never real.

To breach it an attacker must, at once, find a known source, forge a signature, fold the κ-witness, stay within a budget it was never granted, and send something never seen — several independent locks at the same node, not one wall with one breach. The full seven-defence framing (mesh quarantine, bonded-pair cross-check, compression cipher, self-heal) is the design; what is measured here is the signed-packet gate and one grown detector.

What was sealed first

Sealed, committed and pushed before the battery was run. SENTINEL must drop every attack it has never seen while passing every valid packet; the grown detector is evolved on training streams and measured on held-out streams. Published whichever way it lands.

The question. Does the structural gate — verify the signature before the six bytes are ever parsed, budget ≤ the capability lattice, at κ, unseen — catch every one of eight attack shapes with zero false passes, and does a detector GROWN against training streams beat a hand-set one on held-out hostile streams?

The gate. verify the Ed25519 signature over sourceId+payload BEFORE unpack is ever called (an unknown source is dropped before verify; a forged or tampered packet is dropped before parse), then unpack (bad-length, off-κ), source nibble must match, budget ≤ the lattice cap, resources ⊆ the grant, and the signature must be unseen (replay).

The grown detector. training and held-out streams (60 / 60, seeded) of individually-valid packets: scans, drains and bursts are attacks, small focused streams are legit. The detector (a weight per feature n/fanout/budgetSum/dominance/resourceSpread and a threshold) is grown on the training streams only (2000 seeded tries, fitness = catch rate with zero false passes) and measured on the held-out streams against the hand-set baseline {"weights":[0,1,1,0,0],"threshold":0.4}.
