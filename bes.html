Why this generalizes (Gary W. Floyd's GEP-BES)

Identity is insufficient. Behavior is authoritative. "Are you behaving like yourself?" — the gate models the operator, not the attacks, so it never learns an attack boundary to overfit. Attackers fail by being overly regular (scripted — a narrow repeated pattern, the wrong distribution) or randomly variant (uncorrelated — flat, no operator peaks). Both read as high deviation from the operator's structured baseline. Credential validity does not override entropy violation.

Honest scope (Gary §8): this performs stability regulation — whether behavior matches the operator — not morality, intent, or malware classification. The three domains (network / behavioral / authorization) and the ALLOW/THROTTLE/STEP-UP/BLOCK/ISOLATE decision are measured here on a seeded synthetic operator; a production baseline forms online over roughly two weeks.
